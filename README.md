# **Adoption of UPI and Cashless Economy in India**

## 📌 Project Overview

This project studies the **adoption of UPI (Unified Payments Interface)** and the **growth of the cashless economy in India** using **survey data, statistical analysis, and a dashboard**.
The goal is to understand how people use UPI, what benefits they experience, and what challenges still exist.

This is a **data-driven academic project** designed to reflect real-world digital payment trends in India.

## 🎯 Objectives

* To analyze how widely UPI is used in India
* To understand customer trust and satisfaction with UPI
* To study whether UPI has reduced the use of cash
* To perform **hypothesis testing** using survey data
* To visualize findings through a **dashboard**

---

## 📊 Dataset

The data was collected using a **survey questionnaire** covering:

* Age group
* Occupation
* Frequency of UPI usage
* Reasons for using UPI
* Security & trust level
* Problems faced
* Preference between Cash and Digital payments

The dataset is stored in the **Dataset folder** in Excel format.

---

## 📈 Dashboard

An interactive **Excel Dashboard** was created to visualize:

* UPI usage frequency
* Payment preference (Cash vs UPI)
* User satisfaction
* Security perception
* Growth of digital payments

The dashboard allows decision-makers to quickly understand UPI adoption trends.

## 📌 Key Findings

* UPI has significantly increased digital transactions in India
* Most users prefer UPI because it is fast and convenient
* Security concerns still exist but trust in UPI is growing
* Cash usage has reduced among frequent UPI users

---

## 📜 Conclusion

The study shows that **UPI has played a major role in promoting a cashless economy in India**.
While challenges such as security and internet availability remain, digital payments are becoming the preferred mode for most users.

This project demonstrates how **data analysis and dashboards** can be used to support economic and financial decisions.
